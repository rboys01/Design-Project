Rules

The goal of ringette is to get the ring into the opposing team's net. Here are 5 main ringette rules to provide an understanding of the game


    • 1. Offside: No player can carry the ring over a blue line, a pass must be made into each zone (also no 2-line passes allowed).
      2. Extended zone: Only 3 players from each team are permitted past the extended zone (or free play) line, not including the goalies.
      3. Crease: Only the goalies are allowed in the crease, no players can go in either crease.
      4. Penalties: Minor penalties include tripping, slashing, interferance, crosschecking, and hooking. All penalties from behind are automatically 4-minute majors.
      5. When there is a delayed penalty for the opposing team, 4 players can enter the extended zone, creating a 4-on-3 oppurtunity.
