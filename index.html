About Ringette

Ringette is a sport played by women and girls in many countries including Canada, U.S., Finland and Sweden. The sport encourages fast pased action, teamwork, and strategy. Explore this page to learn all about the growing sport of ringette. See the equipment, rules, and history on this website. Then, complete the survey!
